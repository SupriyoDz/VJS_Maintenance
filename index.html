About Vedic Jagritii Sangam

Rooted in Vedic Wisdom. Guided by Dharma. Committed to Seva.

Vedic Jagritiii Sangam is an initiative of Anandamoyee Group, dedicated to preserving India's timeless Vedic heritage and bringing its enduring values into contemporary society. Founded on the principles of Dharma, Karma and Seva, the organisation seeks to build a meaningful connection between ancient wisdom and the needs of modern communities.

Vedic Jagritiii Sangam believes that true social progress goes beyond material development. It grows through knowledge, ethical living, cultural awareness, spiritual understanding and a collective commitment to the welfare of humanity. With this vision, the organisation works to promote Vedic philosophy, traditional values, community participation and socially meaningful initiatives that encourage harmony between individuals, society and nature.

One Vision, United in Service

Vedic Jagritii Sangam — An Initiative of Anandamoyee Group.

Vedic Jagritii Sangam is an initiative of Anandamoyee Group, bringing together the timeless principles of Dharma, Karma and Seva with a commitment to spiritual awareness, cultural preservation and community welfare. While Vedic Jagritii Sangam focuses on promoting Vedic knowledge, spiritual and cultural activities, community engagement and service-oriented initiatives, Anandamoyee Group provides the broader vision and organisational foundation that supports its mission. Together, they work towards a shared goal: making traditional wisdom meaningful and accessible to society.

Through this relationship, the values of spirituality, knowledge and social responsibility are reflected in initiatives designed to encourage participation, preserve cultural heritage and contribute to the wellbeing of communities.

  • One Shared Vision

    A common purpose, guided by the values of service and social responsibility.

  • A Connected Approach to Service

    Vedic Jagritii Sangam works to bring people together through spiritual programmes, cultural activities, community outreach and welfare initiatives, in alignment with the broader vision of Anandamoyee Group.

  • A Commitment to Knowledge and Heritage

    The initiative encourages awareness of India's Vedic traditions, cultural values and spiritual heritage, helping connect timeless knowledge with contemporary life.

  • A Community Built on Participation

    Members, volunteers, families and well-wishers are encouraged to participate in activities that promote learning, cooperation, cultural awareness and service to society.

  • A Vision for the Future

    Together, Vedic Jagritii Sangam and Anandamoyee Group aspire to support future initiatives in education, holistic wellbeing, skill development and sustainable community living, in accordance with their evolving plans and programmes.

  • United by Dharma. Inspired by Knowledge. Dedicated to Seva.

    Vedic Jagritii Sangam carries forward its mission as an initiative of Anandamoyee Group, guided by the belief that meaningful progress comes from combining knowledge with action and spiritual values with service to humanity.

President, Vedic Jagritii Sangam Vision Statement

Seva, Karma, Dharma

A Karmayogi Guided by Spiritual Wisdom and Committed to Social Progress Leadership

Dr. Hriday Haldar is a visionary leader, institution-builder and Karmayogi whose journey brings together spiritual devotion, India's traditional knowledge heritage, organisational leadership and a commitment to social wellbeing. As the Founder Chairman and CMD of Anandamoyee Group and President of Vedic Jagritii Sangam, he envisions a society in which people can lead healthier, more harmonious and purposeful lives while remaining connected to nature, cultural heritage and enduring human values. His guiding philosophy is rooted in Dharma, Karma and Seva. For him, spirituality is not limited to personal faith; it is also a source of responsibility, inspiration and meaningful action. His work reflects an aspiration to transform ideas into institutions and traditional wisdom into opportunities for learning, wellbeing and community development.

Dr. Hriday Haldar's vision begins with a fundamental aspiration: to contribute to a society in which people can pursue physical wellbeing, mental balance, meaningful livelihoods and a harmonious relationship with nature. India has a rich heritage of knowledge, culture and intellectual inquiry. From the Vedas and philosophical traditions to Ayurveda, yoga, pranayama, agriculture, arts and education, this heritage represents an important part of the country's civilisational legacy. Dr. Haldar believes that preserving this knowledge requires more than remembering the past. It calls for responsible study, contemporary interpretation and practical initiatives that can address the needs of the present and future. His broader vision seeks to connect traditional knowledge with education, wellbeing, research, skill development and social responsibility. Through the institutions associated with his work, he aspires to encourage a future in which heritage and progress can complement one another.
